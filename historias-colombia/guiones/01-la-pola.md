### Clip 1: Hook
**Prompt:** Selfie-style close-up of Nico holding the camera at arm's length, excited, in a colonial
cobblestone plaza in Santafé de Bogotá in 1817, whitewashed houses with wooden balconies, people in
colonial clothes walking behind him, cloudy mountain morning. Nico speaks in Spanish with a Colombian
Bogotá accent: "¡Quiubo, parceros! La ruana de mi abuela me mandó a Santafé, 1817. ¡Y aquí hay una espía!"

### Clip 2: Meet La Pola
**Prompt:** Inside a candlelit colonial house, La Pola sits sewing a dress by a window, wealthy royalist
ladies chatting nearby. Nico peeks from behind a doorframe and whispers to the camera, in Spanish with a
Colombian Bogotá accent: "Ella es Policarpa Salavarrieta, La Pola. Trabaja de costurera en casas de familias realistas…"

### Clip 3: The secret
**Prompt:** Close-up of La Pola's hands secretly slipping a folded note into a sewing basket while
Spanish officers talk in the background. Cut to Nico, amazed, whispering to the camera in Spanish with a
Colombian Bogotá accent: "…pero mientras cose, escucha todo y les pasa los secretos a los patriotas. ¡Qué berraca!"

### Clip 4: Discovered
**Prompt:** Night, narrow colonial street lit by torches, Spanish soldiers in blue-and-red royalist
uniforms march fast. Nico hides behind a wooden door, worried, whispering in Spanish with a Colombian
Bogotá accent: "Pero los soldados españoles la descubren. Le encuentran una lista de patriotas."

### Clip 5: Her brave words
**Prompt:** Dusk in the main plaza of Santafé, a crowd gathered, La Pola standing tall and defiant
between two soldiers (no violence shown). Nico in the foreground, serious, says softly in Spanish with a
Colombian Bogotá accent: "En la plaza, en vez de quedarse callada, le gritó al pueblo que despertara."

### Clip 6: Why we remember her
**Prompt:** Slow push-in on Nico's face, emotional, golden light, gentle wind moving his ruana, the
plaza blurred behind him. He says in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent: "Por eso el 14 de noviembre
es el Día de la Mujer Colombiana. Por ella."

### Clip 7: Back to the present + call to action
**Prompt:** Present-day Plaza de Bolívar in Bogotá, pigeons, the Capitolio behind, sunny day. Nico
holds up an old Colombian ten-thousand-peso banknote toward the camera, smiling. He says in Spanish with
a Colombian Bogotá accent: "¡Y salía en el billete de diez mil! ¿Ustedes sabían? ¡Síganme pa' más historias! ¡Chao, parceros!"

### Fact notes
- Policarpa Salavarrieta, born in Guaduas around 1795. She was a patriot spy in Santafé who worked as a seamstress.
- Captured in 1817 and executed on **Nov 14, 1817** in the main plaza (today Plaza de Bolívar).
- Her famous words: "¡Pueblo indolente! Cuán distinta sería hoy vuestra suerte si conocierais el precio de la libertad."
- Nov 14 is the Día de la Mujer Colombiana. She appeared on the old $10,000 COP banknote.
