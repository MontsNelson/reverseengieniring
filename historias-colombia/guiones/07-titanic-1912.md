# Episode 7: El Titanic (1912)

**Length:** about 56 s (7 clips × 8 s)
**Big title (0–3 s):** **TITANIC** / **1912** (white, with the year in yellow)
**Hook text:** "Estuve en el Titanic 🚢🧊" ("I was on the Titanic")
**Nico's action:** he reports live from the deck, gets lost in the giant ship, sees the iceberg, and helps a little girl into a lifeboat.
**Nico's outfit (keep it in every clip):** 1912 boy clothes: gray wool newsboy cap, brown tweed jacket a bit tight on his belly, white shirt, knee-length knickerbockers, long socks, brown boots. Always holding the NICO microphone.

**Recurring characters:**
- *Captain Edward Smith: older man, full white beard, dark navy captain's uniform with gold buttons and cap.*
- *Little girl: about 6, blonde curls, white dress, big bow, holding a teddy bear.*

Start every prompt with the **Nico character block** from `PLAN.md`. Every prompt ends with:
*no subtitles, no on-screen text, no background music, no blood, no dead bodies.*
**Tone:** fun and amazed at the start, calm and respectful at the end. Never make jokes during the sinking.

---

### Clip 1: Hook
**Prompt:** Handheld news-camera close-up of chubby little Nico holding his NICO reporter microphone on
the crowded dock of Southampton, April 10, 1912. The gigantic black-and-white Titanic with four huge
yellow-and-black funnels towers behind him, passengers in hats waving, steam and seagulls. Thumbs-up,
cheeky smile. He says in Spanish with a Colombian paisa accent from Medellín: "¡Quiubo, pues,
parceros! Reportando desde Inglaterra, 1912. ¡Este es el barco más grande del mundo!"

### Clip 2: "Unsinkable"
**Prompt:** On the sunny top deck, elegant first-class passengers stroll by in long dresses and top
hats. Nico walks next to a big lifeboat hanging from its davits, knocking on the ship's railing proudly.
He says in Spanish with a Colombian paisa accent from Medellín: "Dicen que es imposible que se hunda.
¡Ave María, qué lujo, parce!"

### Clip 3: Lost inside the ship
**Prompt:** The famous grand staircase with a glass dome and golden lights. Nico runs down the stairs,
out of breath, belly bouncing, looking left and right. Then a quick shot of him popping out of the
wrong door into the hot, noisy boiler room full of stokers shoveling coal. He says in Spanish with a
Colombian paisa accent from Medellín: "¡Me perdí! Este barco es como un centro comercial flotando…"

### Clip 4: The iceberg
**Prompt:** Night, April 14, 1912, freezing, flat black sea full of stars, Nico's breath visible. He
stands at the railing with his microphone, then his eyes go huge as an enormous white iceberg slides past
the side of the ship, scraping it, chunks of ice falling on the deck. He whispers in Spanish with a
Colombian paisa accent from Medellín: "11:40 de la noche… ¡parceros, un iceberg! ¡Nos chocamos!"

### Clip 5: Not enough lifeboats
**Prompt:** On the cold deck at night, crew members in white life jackets lower lifeboats, people in coats
and life jackets lining up calmly, the captain giving orders. Nico, wearing a big white life jacket that
barely closes around his belly, says seriously in Spanish with a Colombian paisa accent from Medellín:
"El problema: había botes salvavidas pa' poquita gente. Más o menos pa' la mitad."

### Clip 6: Nico helps
**Prompt:** Nico takes the hand of the little girl with the teddy bear and gently helps her into a
lifeboat, where her mother hugs her. He waves goodbye and the lifeboat is lowered toward the dark
water. In the background, musicians in suits play violins calmly on the deck. Nico speaks softly in
Spanish with a Colombian paisa accent from Medellín: "Dicen que los músicos siguieron tocando hasta el
final. Qué valientes."

### Clip 7: Discovery + call to action
**Prompt:** Calm, respectful mood. Deep underwater, the rusty bow of the Titanic wreck on the dark ocean
floor, lit by a submarine's lights. Then cut to Nico in a museum in front of a huge Titanic model, taking
off his cap. He says in Spanish with a Colombian paisa accent from Medellín: "Lo encontraron en 1985, a
casi 4 kilómetros de profundidad. ¿Cierto que no sabían? ¡Síganme pa' más historias!"

### Fact notes
- The Titanic sailed from Southampton on **April 10, 1912**. It hit an iceberg at about **11:40 p.m. on April 14**
  and sank at about 2:20 a.m. on April 15.
- About 2,200 people were on board and more than 1,500 died. The 20 lifeboats could carry about 1,178
  people, roughly half of those aboard.
- It was widely described as "practically unsinkable". The ship's band is reported to have kept playing.
- The RMS Carpathia rescued about 700 survivors. The wreck was found in **1985** (Robert Ballard's
  expedition), about 3,800 m deep.
- Getting lost, the boiler room and helping the girl are fiction for Nico. Keep the sinking
  respectful.
