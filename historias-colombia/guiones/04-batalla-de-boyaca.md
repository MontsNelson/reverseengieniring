# Episode 4: La Batalla de Boyacá

**Length:** about 56 s (7 clips × 8 s)
**Hook text (0–2 s):** "Cruzaron los Andes SIN ZAPATOS 🥶⛰️" ("They crossed the Andes WITHOUT SHOES")

**Recurring characters:**
- *Simón Bolívar: thin man in his 30s, dark curly hair and sideburns, dark blue military jacket with gold epaulettes.*
- *Juan José Rondón: strong llanero colonel, dark skin, wide-brimmed hat, holding a long lance, on horseback.*

Start every prompt with the **Nico character block**. Every prompt ends with:
*no subtitles, no on-screen text, no background music.* Keep battles epic but not gory (no blood).

---

### Clip 1: Hook
**Prompt:** Selfie-style close-up of Nico shivering in a freezing, foggy Andean páramo full of frailejón
plants, 1819, a long line of ragged soldiers marching behind him in the mist, wind howling. Teeth
chattering, he says in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent: "¡Quiubo, parceros! ¡Qué frío! Estoy
en el páramo de Pisba, 1819, con el ejército de Bolívar."

### Clip 2: The crossing
**Prompt:** Close shots of tired patriot soldiers walking barefoot on cold rocks, wrapped in blankets,
helping each other up the steep path. Nico, impressed: "Cruzaron los Andes desde los Llanos, ¡muchos sin zapatos! Imagínense este frío."
Spoken in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent.

### Clip 3: Pantano de Vargas
**Prompt:** Green marshy battlefield, Bolívar on horseback turns to Rondón and shouts dramatically. Nico in
the foreground, excited, imitating the shout, in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent: "En el Pantano
de Vargas, Bolívar le grita a Rondón: '¡Coronel, salve usted la patria!'"

### Clip 4: The lancers' charge
**Prompt:** Epic slow-motion shot, Rondón and a small group of llanero lancers on horses charging across
the field, lances forward, hooves splashing through mud, thunder of horses. Nico cheering: "¡Y Rondón,
con poquitos lanceros, carga contra los españoles! ¡Y lo logran!" Spoken in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent.

### Clip 5: Puente de Boyacá
**Prompt:** Morning, a small stone bridge over a river in green hills, patriot troops advancing toward
royalist troops in formation. Nico whispers seriously to the camera, in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá
accent: "7 de agosto. Puente de Boyacá. Bolívar y Santander atacan al ejército español."

### Clip 6: Victory
**Prompt:** After the battle, patriot soldiers celebrating and raising their hats, a royalist general
in a fancy uniform being led away as a prisoner. Nico jumping with joy: "La batalla duró como dos horas,
¡y capturaron al general español Barreiro! ¡Ganamos!" Spoken in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent.

### Clip 7: Present day + call to action
**Prompt:** Present-day Puente de Boyacá monument area, sunny, Colombian flags waving in the wind. Nico
proudly wraps a small Colombian flag around his shoulders, in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent:
"Por eso el 7 de agosto es fiesta en Colombia. ¿Ya sabían? ¡Síganme pa' más historias!"

### Fact notes
- The Liberation Campaign crossed the Páramo de Pisba (July 1819) from the Llanos. Many soldiers were barefoot, and many died of cold.
- Pantano de Vargas (July 25, 1819): Bolívar's famous line to Juan José Rondón, "¡Coronel, salve usted la patria!"
- Battle of Boyacá: **Aug 7, 1819**, about two hours. Royalist commander José María Barreiro was captured.
  Bolívar entered Santafé on Aug 10. Aug 7 is a national holiday.
