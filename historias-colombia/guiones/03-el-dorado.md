# Episode 3: El Dorado y la Laguna de Guatavita

**Length:** about 56 s (7 clips × 8 s)
**Hook text (0–2 s):** "¡Me volví DORADO! ✨😂" ("I turned GOLDEN!")
**Nico's action:** he gets covered in gold dust by accident, rows the raft, and hides from the conquistadors.

**Recurring character, the Muisca cacique:** *young Muisca chief, long straight black hair, gold nose
ornament, feather headdress, calm and kind.*

Start every prompt with the **Nico character block**. Every prompt ends with:
*no subtitles, no on-screen text, no background music, no blood.*

---

### Clip 1: Hook
**Prompt:** Selfie-style close-up of chubby little Nico at dawn by a round misty lagoon surrounded by
green Andean mountains. He rubs his cold hands, amazed, and says in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent:
"¡Quiubo, parceros! Estoy en la Laguna de Guatavita, hace 500 años. ¡Vamos a buscar El Dorado!"

### Clip 2: Not a city
**Prompt:** Muisca people in white cotton mantas walk to the lagoon shore with torches and small gold
figures. Nico follows behind them, tripping on his ruana, and says to the camera in Spanish with a Colombian
Bogotá accent: "Pensé que era una ciudad de oro… ¡pero es una ceremonia de los muiscas!"

### Clip 3: Golden Nico
**Prompt:** The cacique is being covered in shimmering gold dust when a gust of wind blows a big golden
cloud onto Nico. Nico is now completely golden, sneezes a little golden puff, and the cacique laughs.
Nico says in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent: "¡Uy! ¡Ahora yo también soy El Dorado! ¡Achís!"

### Clip 4: The raft
**Prompt:** Wide cinematic shot, the golden cacique standing on a reed raft gliding across the misty lagoon,
and golden Nico at the back paddling hard with a tiny oar, puffing his cheeks. Gold pieces and green emeralds
drop into the water. Nico says in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent: "Le ofrecen oro y esmeraldas a
los dioses… ¡y yo remando, parceros!"

### Clip 5: The conquistadors
**Prompt:** Spanish conquistadors in metal helmets appear on the hill, eyes shining greedily at the gold.
Golden Nico dives into the tall reeds and only his shiny eyes peek out. He whispers in Spanish with a
Colombian Bogotá accent: "¡Llegaron los españoles! Se volvieron locos buscando la ciudad de oro."

### Clip 6: Draining the lake
**Prompt:** Workers with picks and shovels dig a deep cut into the rim of the lagoon, 16th century. Nico,
still a bit golden, shakes his finger at them. He says in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent:
"¡Hasta quisieron secar la laguna! Y todavía se ve el hueco que le hicieron."

### Clip 7: Present day + call to action
**Prompt:** Present-day museum, dark room, a small golden raft with tiny figures glowing in a glass case.
Nico (normal color again, with a little gold glitter left on his nose) points at it, delighted. He says in
Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent: "¡La Balsa Muisca, en el Museo del Oro! ¡Toda de oro! ¡Síganme, parceros!"

### Fact notes
- The El Dorado legend comes from the Muisca ritual at Laguna de Guatavita: a new ruler covered in
  gold dust made offerings of gold and emeralds from a raft.
- Spaniards tried to drain the lake more than once. Antonio de Sepúlveda's cut (1580s) is still visible.
- The Balsa Muisca was found in 1969 near Pasca (Cundinamarca) and is in the Museo del Oro, Bogotá.
