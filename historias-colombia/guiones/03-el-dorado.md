# Episode 3: El Dorado y la Laguna de Guatavita

**Length:** about 56 s (7 clips × 8 s)
**Hook text (0–2 s):** "El Dorado NO era una ciudad 🤯✨" ("El Dorado was NOT a city")

**Recurring character, the Muisca cacique:** *young Muisca chief, long straight black hair, gold
nose ornament and feather headdress, calm and dignified.*

Start every prompt with the **Nico character block**. Every prompt ends with:
*no subtitles, no on-screen text, no background music.*

---

### Clip 1: Hook
**Prompt:** Selfie-style close-up of Nico at dawn beside a round misty lagoon surrounded by green Andean
mountains, mist over the water, birdsong. Amazed, he says in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent:
"¡Quiubo, parceros! Estoy en la Laguna de Guatavita, hace como 500 años. ¿Saben qué es El Dorado?"

### Clip 2: Not a city
**Prompt:** Muisca people in white cotton mantas walking toward the lagoon shore carrying torches and
small gold figures, smoke from incense. Nico shakes his head with a smile, in Spanish with a Colombian
Bogotá accent: "No era una ciudad de oro. ¡Era una ceremonia de los muiscas!"

### Clip 3: Covered in gold
**Prompt:** Close-up of the Muisca cacique being gently covered with shimmering gold dust from head to
toe, glowing in the torchlight. Nico, eyes wide: "Al nuevo cacique lo cubrían de polvo de oro, de pies a cabeza. ¡Brillaba como un sol!"
Spoken in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent.

### Clip 4: The raft
**Prompt:** Wide cinematic shot, the golden cacique on a reed raft gliding to the center of the misty
lagoon, dropping gold pieces and green emeralds into the water, people singing on the shore. Nico
narrates in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent: "Iba en una balsa al centro de la laguna y les
ofrecía oro y esmeraldas a los dioses."

### Clip 5: The Spanish arrive
**Prompt:** Spanish conquistadors in metal helmets listening to the story around a campfire, their eyes
lighting up greedily. Nico rolls his eyes at the camera, in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent:
"Cuando los españoles oyeron eso, ¡se volvieron locos buscando la ciudad de oro!"

### Clip 6: Draining the lake
**Prompt:** Workers digging a deep cut into the rim of the lagoon with picks and shovels, 16th century.
Nico, hands on his head: "¡Hasta trataron de secar la laguna! Y todavía se ve el corte que le hicieron."
Spoken in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent.

### Clip 7: Present day + call to action
**Prompt:** Present-day museum, dark room, a small intricate golden raft with tiny figures glowing in a
glass case. Nico points at it, delighted, in Spanish with a Colombian Bogotá accent: "Esta es la Balsa
Muisca, en el Museo del Oro. ¡Toda de oro! ¡Síganme pa' más historias, parceros!"

### Fact notes
- The El Dorado legend comes from the Muisca ritual at Laguna de Guatavita: a new ruler covered in
  gold dust made offerings from a raft.
- Spaniards tried to drain the lake more than once. Antonio de Sepúlveda's cut (1580s) is still visible.
- The "Balsa Muisca" was found in 1969 near Pasca (Cundinamarca) and is in the Museo del Oro, Bogotá.
