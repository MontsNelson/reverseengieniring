# Nico reference images: prompts for Google Flow (image mode / Nano Banana)

Generate these first, then pick the best ones and keep them as Nico's **Ingredients** for every video.
(Nico is an original character inspired by the "kid history reporter" format. Don't upload or
copy another creator's character as a reference.)

## 1. Main portrait (the "official" Nico face)
Photorealistic portrait photo, vertical 9:16, shallow depth of field, warm natural light. An 8-year-old
chubby Colombian boy from Medellín, round face, full rosy cheeks, round belly, warm light-brown skin, big
dark-brown eyes, short curly black hair, small gap where a front tooth is missing, cheeky confident
half-smile. He holds a black foam TV reporter microphone with a cube-shaped mic flag in yellow, blue and
red with the word "NICO". He gives a thumbs-up to the camera. Simple white shirt. Plain soft gray
background. Waist-up.

## 2. Character sheet (consistency)
Same boy as the reference image, character turnaround sheet: front view, three-quarter view, side
view, full body, standing, holding the NICO microphone, plain light background, photorealistic,
consistent face and body.

## 3. Era test: colonial Bogotá 1810
Same boy as the reference image, photorealistic cinematic film still, vertical 9:16. He wears 1810
criollo boy clothes (white high-collar shirt, brown vest, dark breeches, small black felt hat) and holds
the NICO microphone, reporting to the camera with a thumbs-up in a busy colonial market plaza in
Santafé de Bogotá, cathedral and wooden balconies behind, warm golden color grade.

## 4. Era test: Columbus ship 1492
Same boy as the reference image, photorealistic cinematic film still, vertical 9:16. He wears a
cream ship's boy tunic and a red wool sailor's cap and holds the NICO microphone on the deck of a
15th-century wooden ship, huge white sails with red crosses, blue ocean, sailors behind him.

**Tips**
- Keep the words **"Same boy as the reference image"** and attach image #1 for every later image.
- If the mic flag text comes out garbled, don't worry: add a clean "NICO" logo sticker in CapCut, or
  ask for "mic flag with no text" and add your logo in the edit.
- Image #3 or #4 can be used directly as the **first frame** in Frames to Video.
